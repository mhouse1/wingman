# Code Review 007 - wingman.log anomaly review and performance histogram

| Status | Date | Wingman Version |
|---|---|---|
| Closed | 2026-05-10 | 1.6.6 |

## Scope

Review of runtime behavior from wingman.log plus performance distribution analysis.

- Log source: wingman.log
- Window analyzed: approximately 2 hours (04:20 to 06:26)
- OCR cycle samples: 3,955
- Health OCR samples: 3,258
- Histogram artifact: docs/performance/wingman-performance-histogram.png

## Findings

### High

1. Health OCR spike rate is high and persistent.
- 170 spikes over 300 out of 3,258 health reads (5.2 percent).
- Maximum observed spike: 2,422,544.
- Spike pattern is consistent with prepended digit noise (examples include 7224, 22224, 72244, 127447).
- Risk: unstable health-based logic and future false-state actions if guardrails are not added.

2. Respawn detection in eject_and_dive times out every time.
- 10 out of 10 eject_and_dive flows hit the 120 second timeout warning.
- Recovery still happens later, indicating respawn likely occurs but is not detected by the polling path in time.
- Risk: delayed recovery flow, noisy warnings, and potential mission-state desynchronization.

### Medium

3. OCR pipeline performance is generally healthy with a small slow tail.
- Mean total OCR cycle: 0.65s.
- Median total OCR cycle: 0.59s.
- 88 percent of cycles are under 1.0s.
- 2 percent of cycles exceed 1.5s.
- Interpretation: baseline throughput is acceptable; tail latency should continue to be monitored.

### Low

4. No ERROR log lines observed in the reviewed window.
- Warnings were dominated by eject_and_dive timeout messages.
- State transition flow remained clean across repeated mission loops.

## Evidence Snapshot

- Health spikes over threshold: 170 / 3,258 (5.2 percent).
- Largest spike: 2,422,544.
- Eject timeout warnings: 10 occurrences.
- OCR totals: n=3,955, mean=0.65s, median=0.59s.

## Recommended Actions

1. Implement ADR 030 health ceiling filter in analyzer health processing path to reject implausible OCR spikes.
2. Investigate and fix eject_and_dive respawn detection data path so timeout is no longer the default outcome.
3. Keep histogram generation as a recurring validation artifact after performance-sensitive changes.

## Resolutions

| Finding | Resolution |
|---------|------------|
| High-1 — Health OCR spikes | Fixed 2026-05-10 — ADR 030 rolling-window ceiling filter implemented in `_apply_health_ceiling_filter`; resets on `on_enter_GAME_LOBBY` and `on_enter_GAME_BATTLE` |
| High-2 — eject_and_dive respawn timeout | Fixed 2026-05-10 — `_is_respawning()` now reads `analyzer.game_battle_alive` (health-based, reliable) instead of `_ocr_cache['result']` (respawn-text OCR, frequently missed) |
