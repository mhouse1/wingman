## R1 — INCOMING crop region bleeds adjacent HUD text

**Severity:** Medium  
**Risk to fix:** High (crop coordinate change could reduce missile-detection sensitivity)

### Observation

The INCOMING OCR region consistently captures readable text from HUD elements that are
not the missile-warning banner. Two distinct patterns appear in the log:

**Pattern A — "TURN TO BATTLE" game mode button** (late in battle, ~19:04):
```
19:04:17,536 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE'
19:04:19,115 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE'
19:04:20,890 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE'
19:04:22,305 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE'
19:04:23,676 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='TURNTOBATTLE'
```

**Pattern B — Cockpit/warning HUD text** (during active combat):
```
18:59:11,350 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='CKWARNI', binary_otsu_up_1p4='CKWARNI'
18:59:24,668 [DEBUG] No match in INCOMING region — raw OCR: gray_up_1p4='CKWARMIE', binary_otsu_up_1p4='CKWAN'
```

Both are correctly rejected (no match to INCOMING keyword list), so there are no false
positives today. However, both variants appear on multiple consecutive frames, confirming
they are stable HUD elements, not transient OCR noise.

### Root cause

The `INCOMING` crop coordinates partially overlap with:
- The "TURN TO BATTLE" mission-mode indicator (visible late in GAME_BATTLE)
- An abbreviated cockpit/mode indicator text (likely "COCKWARNING" or "CHECK WARNING")

### Risk of fixing

Shrinking the crop risks missing the top or sides of the real INCOMING banner. The INCOMING
banner's exact pixel position relative to the crop is unknown; no screenshot capture was
taken during a live missile event to verify the true extent of the banner.

### Recommended investigation

1. Press `V` (screenshot hotkey) during a live missile-warning event to capture the
   frame. Overlay the current crop rectangle to see how much margin exists above/below
   the INCOMING text.
2. Compare the Y-axis position of the TURNTOBATTLE and CKWARNI text against the real
   INCOMING banner to determine if a vertical crop trim is safe.
3. Alternatively, add "TURNTOBATTLE" and "CKWARN" as suppression prefixes in the
   INCOMING keyword filter (reject any match that starts with these strings even if a
   later OCR pass matches something). Lower risk than moving the crop.

## R2 — Health ceiling ratchets down within a life; mid-life OCR noise can lock it low

**Severity:** Low–Medium  
**Risk to fix:** Medium (HEALTH_SPIKE_FACTOR and HEALTH_WINDOW_SIZE tune a filter with no ground truth)

### Observation

In this session, the health ceiling anchored at 84 for the entire second life (18:58:43–19:00:08 — approximately 1m25s), during which every true health read of 224 was rejected:

```
18:58:43,523 [WARNING] Analyzer: Health OCR spike rejected: 224 (ceiling=84, factor=1.5)
...  (repeated every ~1.3s)
19:00:08,030 [WARNING] Analyzer: Health OCR spike rejected: 244 (ceiling=84, factor=1.5)
```

The respawn-reset fix (implemented this session) clears `_health_window` and
`_health_ceiling` on the False→True alive transition, which will prevent this scenario on
future runs. However, a secondary risk remains:

If OCR reads a spuriously low digit during a life — for example, reading "24" instead of
"224" (one digit dropped) — and the window fills with low values before the error clears,
the ceiling will ratchet downward and begin rejecting true readings. The window size and
spike factor determine how quickly this recovers.

### Current parameters

| Parameter | Value | Effect |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| `HEALTH_WINDOW_SIZE` | 10 | 10 frames needed to evict one bad reading |
| `HEALTH_SPIKE_FACTOR` | 1.5 | Ceiling = max(window) × 1.5 |

### Risk of fixing

Neither parameter has a ground-truth calibration. Increasing the window makes recovery
slower; decreasing it makes the filter more reactive to noise. Increasing the factor makes
the filter more permissive; decreasing it rejects more spikes. The interaction with the
now-reset respawn window needs a run or two to validate before tuning.

### Recommended next step

Run 2–3 sessions with the respawn reset in place and review `spike rejected` warning
counts. If ceiling ratchet within a life causes significant rejection runs (>20 consecutive
frames), revisit HEALTH_WINDOW_SIZE (reduce to 5) or HEALTH_SPIKE_FACTOR (increase to 2.0).
