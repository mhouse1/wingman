# Mission — F-111 (mission_f111)

| Status | Date       | Wingman Version |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Draft  | 2026-09-25 | 1.8.11          |

`mission_su30` with one addition: the F-111's wing sweep, toggled with `w`.
The wings are swept for the climb and unswept when the aircraft descends back
to the level-off altitude. Everything not listed below follows
`docs/missions/README.md` (shared behavior in section 3, defaults in section
2.2).

Implement per `docs/missions/README.md`.

## name

mission_f111

## sequence

* nose up on battle starting or respawn, and as the climb starts press `w` once to sweep the wings
* boresight engage mode only
* do not switch weapons until the current weapon runs out, then switch to the secondary weapon
* after reaching 3000 m altitude set nose angle to -10 degrees
* when the altitude descends back to 3000 m, press `w` once to unsweep the wings; wait at most 30 seconds, and on timeout unsweep anyway
* activate pursuit mode

## notes

* **Same as SU-30 otherwise.** Bullets 1 to 4 and 6 are `su30.md`'s sequence, and the engagement, weapon switch, nose-angle step and pursuit hand-off match `mission_su30` (ADR 144). Only the two `w` presses and the wait in bullet 5 are new.
* **`w` is a toggle.** The game binds it as `ToggleWingSweep` (job aid 011), so each press flips the wings. Track the swept state and press once per bullet (README section 5, rule 8). The wings are unswept at spawn (assumed; confirm on the first live trial). Reset the tracked state where the rest of the per-life state resets, `stop_eject_sequence`, which runs on every respawn and match end. A mission restart within the same life (after the disengage roll, or `u` after a manual takeover) must not press `w` for bullet 1 while the wings are already swept.
* **Press `w` through the controller.** `Controller.wingsweep()` already exists and goes through `_execute_key_press` (README section 5, rule 9). Pass a short tap (0.1 s, as `activate_special_weapon` does) rather than its 0.5 s default unless there is a reason not to, and record the choice in the ADR.
* **Units.** Altitudes are meters, as the HUD reads them and as `su30.md`'s 3000 is. (The original request said 3000 ft; decided 2026-09-25 that meters was meant.)
* **Altitude floor: extend ADR 147 to this mission (decided 2026-09-25).** ADR 147 gives `mission_su30` a 3000 m floor and stands the armed sustain climb aside, but the code checks for the name `su30` (`_su30_flies_own_altitude`). Without the same exception `mission_f111` gets the tree's 4000 m floor and the sustain band, which restart the climb just after the level-off, as they did for su30 before ADR 147. The operator has chosen to extend the exception: `f111_mission.alt_floor_m: 3000`, read the same way, applied while `mission_f111` is the last launched mission. This is a shared-behavior change made on the operator's decision (README default 8). Generalise the su30 check rather than adding a parallel copy, leave su30's behavior and ADR 147's tests unchanged, and keep the ADR 147 config test's relation (floor at or below the level-off altitude) for the new block.
* **The unsweep wait is inside the mission.** Bullet 5 runs before the pursuit hand-off and delays it by at most 30 s, so no thread outlives the mission (README section 5, rule 5). Detect the descent on fresh altitude samples only (rule 7): a fresh reading at or below 3000 m after the level-off. With the floor also at 3000 m, the floor's climb will start just after the crossing; the unsweep does not wait for it. Unsweeping on timeout means the chase always starts with the wings in their spawn position.
* **Cancelled while swept.** If the mission ends between bullets 1 and 5 (manual takeover, End key, missiles empty, disengage), do not press `w` from the `finally`: after a takeover the key is suppressed anyway, and a death resets the wings. Log that the wings were left swept.
* **Config.** The level-off altitude, the unsweep altitude, the floor and the 30 s unsweep timeout go in an `f111_mission` block, separate from `su30_mission`, so retuning one jet does not retune the other (as `jas39_mission.turn_guard_s` does for J20). Copy the su30 angle-step numbers into it with the same comments.
* **Launch.** No hotkey of its own: add `f111` to the `mission.default_mission` choices (README section 2.2, defaults 1 to 3). The shipped default stays as it is.
* **Live trial.** Check for the two wing-sweep lines per life in order (sweep at step 1, unsweep at step 5), no `target alt 5000` climb after the level-off, and `ALTITUDE FLOOR` warnings citing 3000 m, not 4000 m.
