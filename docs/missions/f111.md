# Mission — F-111 (mission_f111)

| Status | Date       | Wingman Version |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Draft  | 2026-09-25 | 1.8.11          |

`mission_su30` with one addition: the F-111's wing sweep, toggled with `w`.
The wings are swept for the climb and unswept when the aircraft descends back
to the level-off altitude. Everything not listed below follows
`docs/missions/README.md` (shared behavior in section 3, defaults in section
2.2).

Implement per `docs/missions/README.md`.

## name

mission_f111

## sequence

* nose up on battle starting or respawn, and as the climb starts press `w` once to sweep the wings
* boresight engage mode only
* do not switch weapons until the current weapon runs out, then switch to the secondary weapon
* after reaching 3000 altitude set nose angle to -10 degrees
* when the altitude descends back to 3000, press `w` once to unsweep the wings
* activate pursuit mode

## notes

* **Same as SU-30 otherwise.** Bullets 1 to 4 and 6 are `su30.md`'s sequence, and the engagement, weapon switch, nose-angle step and pursuit hand-off match `mission_su30` (ADR 144). Only the two `w` presses are new.
* **`w` is a toggle.** The game binds it as `ToggleWingSweep` (job aid 011), so each press flips the wings. Track the swept state for the life and press once per bullet (README section 5, rule 8). Assumption: the wings are unswept at spawn. A mission restart within the same life (after the disengage roll, or `u` after a manual takeover) must not press `w` again if the wings are already swept.
* **Press `w` through the controller.** `Controller.wingsweep()` already exists and goes through `_execute_key_press` (README section 5, rule 9); no new key helper is needed.
* **Units.** The altitude HUD reads meters, and `su30.md`'s 3000 is 3000 m. This spec reads 3000 the same way. The request said 3000 ft (about 914 m); if feet was meant, the number here must change.
* **Altitude floor.** ADR 147's 3000 m floor and the stand-aside of the sustain climb apply to `mission_su30` only: the code checks for the name `su30`. Without the same exception, `mission_f111` gets the tree's 4000 m floor and the armed sustain band, which restart the climb just after the level-off, as they did for su30 before ADR 147. Extending that exception to `f111` is a change to shared behavior (README section 2.2, default 8): report the options before editing it.
* **The descent may not come.** With the floor at 3000 m, the -10 degree step may never take the aircraft back down to 3000 before the hand-off. The wait for bullet 5 is bounded (README default 5). Open: on timeout, should the mission hand off to pursuit with the wings still swept, or unsweep anyway? And should the wait delay the pursuit hand-off, or run alongside it?
* **Config.** The 3000 m trigger is the same number as `su30_mission.climb_alt_m` but goes in its own `f111_mission` block, so retuning one jet does not retune the other (as `jas39_mission.turn_guard_s` does for J20).
* **Launch.** No hotkey of its own: set `mission.default_mission: f111` (README section 2.2, defaults 1 to 3).
